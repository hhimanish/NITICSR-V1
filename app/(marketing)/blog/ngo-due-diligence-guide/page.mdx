# How to Do NGO Due Diligence Before You Fund a Partner

A strong cause-area fit is the easy part of choosing an NGO partner. The part that protects
you at audit time is due diligence — and it's worth doing consistently, before every
engagement, not just for the large ones.

## Registration and statutory status

Confirm the NGO's legal registration (society, trust, or Section 8 company), and check for
12A and 80G registration where relevant to your structure. If the engagement involves
foreign-sourced funds, FCRA status matters too. For CSR routing specifically, confirm CSR-1
registration on the MCA portal.

## Financial health, not just mission fit

Ask for recent audited financials or annual reports. You're not evaluating profitability —
you're checking that the organization has functioning financial controls and isn't
operating in a way that would make fund tracing difficult later.

## Track record and references

Look for evidence of prior projects actually delivered: beneficiary numbers, government or
other corporate partnerships, third-party assessments if available. A compelling pitch deck
is not evidence of delivery.

## Governance basics

A functioning board or governing body, clear leadership, and no obvious conflicts of
interest (for example, funds flowing back to related parties) are baseline checks, not
extra credit.

## Documentation habits

Ask how the NGO documents spend and impact today. An organization with weak reporting
habits will make your CSR-2 disclosure harder, regardless of how good their on-ground work
is.

## Why this belongs in your matching workflow, not after it

Doing these checks after you've already committed budget puts you in a weak position if
something surfaces late. NITICSR's Verification Vault is built to front-load this — so
verification happens before a partner is even recommended, not after a relationship has
already started.

*This guide is educational and general — treat it as a starting checklist, not a complete
legal or financial due diligence framework. Consult your compliance team for engagement-
specific requirements.*
