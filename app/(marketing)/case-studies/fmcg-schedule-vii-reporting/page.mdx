# Making Schedule VII reporting a byproduct of the work

## The situation

An FMCG company's CSR-2 filing each year meant weeks of reconstructing
records: pulling spend data from finance, beneficiary counts from program
teams, and SDG mapping from whoever remembered to track it. Nothing was
wrong with any individual record — they just weren't structured for
disclosure.

## The approach

The CSR team restructured how engagements were documented from the start:
cause category, SDG alignment, and milestone evidence captured as part of
the workflow, not reconstructed afterward.

## The illustrative outcome

- CSR-2 preparation time: **illustrative weeks of reconstruction → records
  already in disclosure-ready shape**
- SDG alignment tracked per project instead of mapped retroactively
- Finance and program teams referencing the same records instead of
  reconciling separate spreadsheets

## Why this matters

Compliance reporting is only painful when it's treated as a separate task
from the work itself. Structuring records around disclosure requirements
from day one turns CSR-2 preparation into an export, not a reconstruction
project.
