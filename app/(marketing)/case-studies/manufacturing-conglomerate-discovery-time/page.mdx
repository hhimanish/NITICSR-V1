# Cutting NGO due-diligence time from weeks to hours

## The situation

A large manufacturing group with CSR obligations across five states was
selecting NGO partners largely through referrals and cold outreach. Each new
partnership required the CSR team to independently verify registration
status, financials, and track record — a process that stretched across
weeks per partner and varied in rigor depending on who ran it.

## The approach

The CSR team consolidated discovery and verification into a single workflow:
a shortlist ranked by cause and geography fit, with verification status
visible before any outreach began.

## The illustrative outcome

- Due-diligence time per partner: **illustrative 3 weeks → 1 day**
- Verification criteria applied consistently across every partner, not
  just the high-budget ones
- Compliance team could review engagement history without requesting
  documents from the CSR team ad hoc

## Why this matters

The bottleneck was never finding NGOs to talk to — it was verifying them
consistently before committing budget. Front-loading verification into
discovery removes that bottleneck rather than adding a review step at the
end.
